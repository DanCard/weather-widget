Most weather apps only tell you what the forecast says. Weather Widget also tells you how right it was.

A clean, resizable home-screen widget that compares yesterday's predictions against what actually happened — so you learn how much to trust today's forecast.

FORECAST vs. ACTUAL
• Past days show the predicted range (yellow bar) next to the measured range, straight from official observation stations
• Browse up to 30 days of history with the widget's arrows
• Accuracy statistics: average error, bias ("forecasts run 2° high"), and days within ±3°F
• Watch a forecast evolve: see how the prediction for a given day changed as it approached

MULTIPLE WEATHER SOURCES
• National Weather Service (US) and Open-Meteo (worldwide) built in — no account needed
• Optional: Silurian, Tomorrow.io, WeatherAPI, Visual Crossing, OpenWeatherMap
• Tap the source name on the widget to switch instantly and compare providers
• Enable, disable, and reorder sources in Settings

A WIDGET THAT RESPECTS YOUR BATTERY
• Two-tier updates: the displayed temperature refreshes from cache with zero extra wakeups
• Network fetches scale back automatically as your battery drains
• Current temperature is interpolated smoothly between hourly data points

DETAILS
• Tap any day for an hourly temperature curve, rain chances, and cloud cover
• Rain amounts and chances, measured where stations report them
• Location by GPS, city/address/ZIP search, or exact coordinates — pin it or let it follow your device
• Resizes from a single cell up to a full forecast strip

No ads. No accounts. Location is used only to fetch weather for where you are.
