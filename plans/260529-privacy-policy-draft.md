# Privacy Policy for Weather Widget

**Effective Date:** May 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Weather Widget**. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information, particularly location data used for local weather forecasts.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Location Data:** If you choose current location during setup, Android provides the app with your approximate or precise location while you are using the setup screen. You can also enter a ZIP code instead.
- **Saved Forecast Area:** The app stores the chosen coordinates locally on your device so scheduled widget refreshes can fetch weather for the same area. The app does not request Android background location permission.
- **Diagnostics:** If Firebase Crashlytics is enabled in the release build, crash reports may include device and app diagnostics used to identify stability problems.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
- **Location Data:** Used only to fetch weather data for the forecast area you configure.
- **Third-Party Weather APIs:** When fetching weather, your coordinates are sent to enabled weather providers, such as National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, Silurian, Tomorrow.io, Visual Crossing, OpenWeatherMap, or WeatherAPI. We do **not** send your name, email address, contacts, photos, or advertising ID to these providers.
- **Crash Diagnostics:** Used for app maintenance and stability improvements, not advertising.

## 4. Data Retention and Security
- **No Accounts:** The app does not require an account.
- **No Developer Server:** We do not store your location history or profile data on our servers.
- **Local Storage:** Settings, cached weather data, diagnostic logs, and any API keys you provide are stored locally on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

## 5. Your Choices
- You can revoke location permissions at any time through your Android System Settings.
- You can choose which weather providers to use within the app's Settings menu.
- You can clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
